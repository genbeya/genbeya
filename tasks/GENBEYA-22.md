Pages and tasks are edited in VS Code's own Markdown editor, which knows nothing of Genbeya's references. Teach it, reusing the link engine. (Genbeya's preview renders them already.)

- [ ] `[[…]]` completion of tasks, pages and files
- [ ] Hover with a task's title and status, a page's title
- [ ] Document links: Ctrl+click opens the task, page or file (lines too)
- [ ] Missing pages and tasks as diagnostics
- [ ] `@mention` completion of the repository's people
- [ ] Open the Genbeya preview beside the editor, scrolled along with it
